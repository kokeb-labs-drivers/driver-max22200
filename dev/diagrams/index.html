The layers, one of them yours¶

Figure description and sources

Top to bottom, each arrow is a call. Your application calls one object, whose public calls are listed from the umbrella header in inc/. The core turns each call into register reads and writes and hands them to the port contract (the CRTP base in inc/*_interface.hpp), which forwards them, at compile time, to the hooks of the port class you write; your port moves the bytes on the bus. The notes on the right say which layers stay the same on every MCU and which one you write. A library without a port contract has only the first two layers.

Drawn from: inc/max22200.hpp public calls; inc/max22200_spi_interface.hpp SpiInterface; figure module docs/diagrams/figures/driver-layers.py.

One call through the layers¶

Figure description and sources

Participants, left to right: Your code, MAX22200 and Your port. The steps run from top to bottom. Your code sends SetChannel(2, true) to MAX22200. A note over MAX22200 says: next = copy of ONCH | 0x04. MAX22200 sends GpioSet(Cmd, ACTIVE) to Your port. MAX22200 sends Transfer(0x81): write STATUS, 8 bit to Your port. Your port returns true, fault byte to MAX22200. MAX22200 sends GpioSet(Cmd, INACTIVE) to Your port. MAX22200 sends Transfer(next) to Your port. Your port returns true to MAX22200. A note over MAX22200 says: copy of ONCH = next. MAX22200 returns Result{Ok} to Your code.

SPI daisy chain¶

Figure description and sources

The MCU drives MOSI into SDI of MAX22200 B; its SDO feeds SDI of MAX22200 A, whose SDO returns to MISO. SCK, CSB and CMD are rails both devices share, so one CSB low phase carries one byte or word for each device.

SPI bus timing¶

Figure description and sources

The five lanes are CMD, CSB, SCK, SDI and SDO, left to right in time. CMD goes high before CSB falls and stays high past the rising edge of CSB; SCK idles low and clocks the eight command bits in on SDI at its rising edges while SDO shifts STATUS[7:0] out, bits 4 to 3 being left out. The table gives each interval with its datasheet value.
