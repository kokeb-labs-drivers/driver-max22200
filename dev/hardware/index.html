Hardware¶

The MAX22200's pins, bus settings, timing and power, as the driver uses them. Everything here is from the datasheet; nothing on this page is verified on hardware with this driver yet (Status and limitations).

Parts¶

Part FAULT register Driver setting
MAX22200A Selective clear on read: a read clears only the flags whose SDI bit is high Config::variant = Variant::Max22200A
MAX22200 Every read clears every flag; a fault that arrives during the read is lost Config::variant = Variant::Max22200 (the default)

The two parts are otherwise the same ("Clearing the FAULT Register", Ordering Information). The chip cannot tell which it is, so the variant is configured; the datasheet recommends the MAX22200A for new designs. The default, Max22200, is correct on both: it never asks for a selective clear, and ClearFaults() sends all ones. Declare Max22200A only when the bill of materials names it and a bench check shows a zero-SDI read leaves a latched flag set (bench steps 5 and 11): a selective clear sent to a MAX22200 clears every flag. The selective clear's SDI byte order follows Figure 8 (LSB byte first), one constant (reg::kFaultClearLsbByteFirst), not verified on hardware.

The eight VM pins are one supply: bypass them with two 0.1 µF ceramics, one at pins 12 and 13 and one at pins 28 and 29, plus 10 µF bulk. TRIGA switches the even channels and TRIGB the odd ones when a channel is set to its trigger pin (trigger_pin); the driver never drives them. SDO and FAULT are open drain: fit a pull-up to the MCU's logic supply. The pinout, the block diagram, the circuit and both tables are drawn from docs/hardware.yml.

Application circuit¶

The Application Diagram, with the parts the Pin Description gives for each pin. One chip drives every load the half bridges support: a high-side solenoid, a low-side solenoid, a latched valve as a full bridge, two outputs in parallel and a brushed DC motor. The diagram has no flyback diodes: when the low-side FET turns off, the high-side FET of the same half bridge turns on and the coil current decays through it (Fixed-Frequency Current Control). TRIGB is grounded; TRIGA is optional.

Control pins¶

Pin CtrlPin Who drives it The driver
CMD CtrlPin::Cmd Your port's GpioSet(), required High for each command byte, low for the data and between accesses
ENABLE CtrlPin::Enable Your port's GpioSet(), required (return true if it is tied high) High in EnsureInitialized(), low in Deinitialize() and after a failed start
FAULT CtrlPin::Fault Your port's GpioRead(), optional Read by ReadFaultPin() only
TRIGA, TRIGB none Your application Never; see the note under Timing

GpioSignal::ACTIVE is the pin's function asserted: CMD and ENABLE high, FAULT low. The port maps it to the level.

Bus settings¶

Setting Value Source
SPI mode 0 (CPOL = 0, CPHA = 0), MSB first "SPI Detailed Description"
Clock Up to 10 MHz (tCLK 100 ns, tCH and tCL 30 ns) Electrical Characteristics, Figure 1
CSB Low for one transfer (1 or 4 bytes), high 30 ns at least between transfers Figure 1
CMD Set at least 20 ns (tCMS) before the CSB falling edge of a command byte, held (tCMH, no value given) after CSB rises; high at the CSB rising edge (p26) Figure 1, p26
SDO Valid 40 ns after the falling SCK edge into 310 Ω and 20 pF; the pull-up's RC limits the rate Electrical Characteristics, note 2
Daisy chain Not supported by this driver (5 MHz in a chain) Figure 5

The driver sends a command byte with CMD high, then the data with CMD low: 4 bytes for a 32-bit access (LSB byte first when writing, MSB byte first when reading), 1 byte for an 8-bit access of bits 31:24. CMD idles high: it goes low only for a data phase and back high right after, so a stray clock is never taken as data. See Architecture.

Timing¶

Symbol Value Where the driver waits
tEN 0.5 ms max, ENABLE rising to SPI ready EnsureInitialized(), after ENABLE goes high (reg::kEnableUs)
tWU 2.5 ms max, ACTIVE = 1 to the outputs active EnsureInitialized(), after each ACTIVE write of the wake loop (reg::kWakeUpUs), every kWakePollUs up to Config::wake_timeout_us
tDIS 2.5 ms max, ENABLE falling to the outputs Hi-Z Not waited: Deinitialize() writes ACTIVE = 0 first, which puts the outputs Hi-Z
tOCP 1.2 µs typ (doubled with SRC) Chip-internal
tOL 200 µs typ, open-load deglitch Chip-internal

EnsureInitialized() blocks for at least tEN + tWU (3 ms), longer while UVM stays set. VDRnCDR and HSnLS change only while every channel is off and TRIGA and TRIGB are low (Programming Procedure, step 5c). The driver checks the channels it switched (ChannelsActive) but cannot see the TRIG pins: keep them low while you change a drive mode or a side, or use Verify::ReadBack, which reports a change the chip ignored.

Below UVLO every channel is Hi-Z and the charge pump stops; the registers survive until V18 falls below the power-on reset threshold, after which every register is back at its reset value and the next call returns DeviceReset without writing to the chip ("Undervoltage Lockout (UVLO)").

Current and heat¶

The OCP threshold is 1.1 A minimum; in CDR the regulation loop limits the current. Do not exceed 1 A RMS per channel, and keep the junction below 125 °C: the safe operating area depends on the board, the supply, the number of channels on, the chopping frequency and SRC (Current Rating, Figures 14 to 17). Thermal shutdown at 145 °C turns every output off; they come back by themselves when the die cools, with the OVT flag set until STATUS is read.

Full-scale current¶

IFS = KFS x 1 V / RREF, with KFS = 15 000 (HFS = 0) or 7 500 (HFS = 1). RREF from 15 kΩ to 100 kΩ gives IFS from 1 A down to 150 mA (HFS halves it). Declare RREF in Config::rref_ohm to use the milliamp conversions (Drive modes). The CDR accuracy is ±6 % from 71 % to 100 % of IFS, ±10 % from 20 % to 70 %, ±15 % from 10 % to 20 % and ±25 % from 5 % to 10 %, with a 1 % RREF: use HFS for small currents.
