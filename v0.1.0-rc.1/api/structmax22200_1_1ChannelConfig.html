Detailed Description

One channel's CFG_CHn register, as codes (Table 11). ChannelConfig{} is the reset value: CDR, low side, every code 0 (no current), 25 kHz, every diagnostic off.

The codes are the datasheet's, so nothing is rounded behind your back; the conversions below (CurrentCode(), HitTimeCode()) turn milliamps and microseconds into them and refuse what the chip cannot do.
